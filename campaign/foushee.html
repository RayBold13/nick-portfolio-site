Rooted in Legacy
Grammy-winning artist Fousheé released her highly anticipated third studio album, Pointy Heights, on September 13, 2024. This 10-track project marks a bold evolution in her sound, embracing influences deeply rooted in her Jamaican heritage. Named after the land her grandfather purchased in Saint Catherine, Jamaica, the album is both a tribute and a personal exploration of identity, legacy, and home.
Stories In The Sound
Tracks like “War” and “Feel Like Home” highlight Fousheé's ability to merge her Caribbean roots with her signature alt-R&B aesthetic. One of the standout moments is “100 Bux”, co-produced with Steve Lacy, which puts a modern spin on classic reggae, cleverly incorporating elements of “Pass the Dutchie” by Musical Youth. Throughout the album, her introspective lyrics explore themes of love, identity, and the ongoing search for peace.
Island Frequencies, Future Sounds
Pointy Heights showcases a dynamic shift from Fousheé’s earlier work, blending reggae, ska, and alternative R&B into a rich, genre-defying soundscape. The album opens with “Birds, Bees”, a track that sets the tone with its infectious reggae groove and whimsical instrumentation. From there, the album unfolds into a vibrant journey that honors Caribbean musical traditions while pushing the boundaries of contemporary R&B.
Landmark in Her Climb
Pointy Heights received critical acclaim for its innovative fusion of styles and Fousheé’s deeply personal storytelling. Critics praised the album's bold artistic direction and emotional resonance. With a concise runtime and no filler tracks, each song delivers a powerful impact, making Pointy Heights a defining moment in Fousheé’s discography and a standout release of 2024.
