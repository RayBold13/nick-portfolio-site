A Confident Step Into the Spotlight
Multi-platinum artist Normani released her highly anticipated debut solo album, Dopamine, on June 14, 2024.
Collaborations That Elevate the Mood
The album features collaborations with Starrah, Gunna, James Blake, and Cardi B, along with contributions from Brandy and Victoria Monét. Notable tracks include the seductive "Big Boy," the introspective "Insomnia," and the sultry "Lights On." The previously released single "Wild Side," featuring Cardi B, also appears on the album.
A Personal Release with Lasting Impact
The album debuted at number 91 on the US Billboard 200 and has sold over 300,000 units in the United States. The album achieved commercial success, debuting at number seven on the Billboard 200 and topping the Top Album Sales chart in the U.S.
